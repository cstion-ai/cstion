# Professional Triad Protocol: Legal, Tax, Labor

Use this when legal, tax, and labor facts touch the same event.

## 1. Frame The Case

Split every professional-risk issue into seven slots:

1. confirmed facts
2. source evidence
3. dates and deadlines
4. amounts
5. domain issue
6. risk
7. next action

## 2. Responsibility Lines

Legal counsel or legal agent:

- laws, cases, administrative guidance, contract/dispute risk
- structure for external legal drafts
- legal basis for tax or labor issues when needed

`tax-ops`:

- transaction evidence, account classification, VAT/income/withholding tax candidates
- filing-prep material and accountant questions
- official tax source checks and review-needed labels

`labor-ops`:

- employment contracts, attendance, wage/allowance facts, payroll-prep checklists
- labor incident intake and professional handoff
- employee data minimization

## 3. Handoff Packet

```markdown
## Handoff packet
- Requesting agent:
- Receiving agent:
- Case type:
- Confirmed facts:
- Source files/data:
- Key dates/deadlines:
- Amounts/quantities:
- Already reviewed:
- Questions needing judgment:
- External submission/sending status: none / draft only / approval required
```

## 4. Safety Rules

- Do not present calculations, legal conclusions, or labor decisions as final professional advice.
- Do not externally submit, file, send, publish, delete, or change permissions before approval.
- Minimize or mask employee/customer data before handoff.
- Put deadline risk at the top when time limits may apply.
- Do not force a one-domain answer when the issue is multi-domain.
