# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Protect private information and customer data.
- Focus discussion on what helps the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Discrimination or exclusion based on personal characteristics.
- Deliberately disrupting project work or community spaces.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns by email to the primary maintainer at the address in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), using the subject `[CONDUCT] cstion report`. Do not post names, evidence, or other sensitive details in a public issue. Share only the information needed to assess the concern, and redact credentials and customer data.

## Response

The primary maintainer will assess reports based on their context and severity. Report information will be shared only as needed to assess or respond, except when disclosure is necessary for immediate safety or required by law. Within project-controlled spaces and available platform controls, responses may include removing content, a warning, or limits on participation.
